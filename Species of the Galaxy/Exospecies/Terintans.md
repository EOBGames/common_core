# The Rise, Zenith, and Fall of the Terintans


## The Coming Apocalypse
As the Terintans came to the space age, a horrifying revelation became increasingly apparant about their homeworld- it was dying, at an alarming rate, as its atmosphere was sloughing off. Generous estimates gave the species a mere 50 years to survive. But, inspired by their faith, the Terintans refused to let this be the end. Vast colony ships, each carrying millions, were built, and sent off into the great unknown to ensure that the species would endure past its home. Centuries later, as each ship arrived, they made a terrible discovery that their methods of communication with the other arks were lost. And so, each ark developed independently, creating new independent states, each claiming to be the sole descendant of their species.

It wasn't until the invention of bluespace communications that their error became clear. And so began a new problem- one for dominance. With each state claiming to be the true heir to the original empire, war seemed inevitable. But, from this chaos, rose a unifier- a new Prophet. In holy conflict she brought the warring empires together under one banner- the Second Empire, and turned the Terintans outward in a new era of religious expansionism. As she grew closer to death, she codified a new religious decree for the species: that there was always to be a Prophet, that when she passed her soul would be reborn anew, and that it was the task of every devout Terintan to find this new Prophet and elevate them to her status. And, that the role of the Terintans was to expand across the stars, until an empire of "supreme evil" would rise against them in a War in the Heavens, where victory would bring forth the Gods themselves into the material world.

Such was the way of it for centuries. At times, disputes arose over the line of succession, resulting in bloody civil wars, but the Second Empire could never be truly broken from within. Instead, it would be a new threat that would be the first true challenge of the Terintans- the Ghrall Empire, a nascent star empire that was growing increasingly close to them in power. Terintan theologists could not ignore the parallels to the theological War in the Heavens, and would eventually declare the Ghrall to be the ultimate evil, that must be cleansed from the face of the universe to usher in the era of the Gods.

The following war was devastating for both empires, but the Ghrall had the worst of it. Turned to increasingly desperate measures to hold back the genocidal Terintans, they would develop a weapon that could ensure the destruction of their enemies- a targeted genetic bioweapon, with a long gestation period- long enough to ensure it would spread far and wide across the Terintan Empire before it awoke and killed them in droves.

The Ghrall would never get to deploy their weapon in defense of their state. Their homeworld fell to the Terintans mere days after its completion. But, the fire of resistance still burned in the hearts of the Ghrall, and the virus was eventually deployed in a terrorist attack by Ghrall Avengers. None of this stopped the genocide. The Ghrall species, for all intents and purposes, was wiped from the face of the universe by the Terintans, who settled in to bask in their victory in the War in the Heavens.

For years after the event, nothing seemed to happen- and then, one day, the Terintans started to get sick. The virus had awoken. First, a simple cold. Then, it got worse. Before long, necrosis set in, and limbs fell off. Eating and drinking became impossible. Eventually, the exhausted body simply gives up, several torturous months after initially falling ill. Fatality rates from an infection were as high as 99.9%. Measures to prevent the spread proved futile, with nearly all worlds across the empire having the disease well established. The zealous Terintans fell into a millenarianist frenzy, turning on each other even as the disease destroyed them. Some lucky uninfected pockets sealed themselves away, trying to preserve the species. Some of these vaults were raided by apocalyptic cultists, decrying them as "avoiding the Gods' plan". Others still sat undisturbed, as the empire died, and entropy set in. One by one, power fell away to these vaults. The Ghrall had their revenge; the Terintans, over the course of decades, died out, leaving their hive worlds as grim tombs for their species.
