# Three Weeks on a God's Face: A Marine's Account of the Battle of Atrakor

## Foreword
The following is an account of the Battle of Atrakor, roughly spanning from initial planetfall to the final bombing of Atrakor, as taken from the memoirs of one Staff Sergeant Rickart "Red" Sullivan of the 41st Helljumper Battalion (*Vikings*), Terran Marine Corps, who was one of the marines assigned to the doomed TBS Rickenbacker.

### Day 1
New deployment. We've reached the Imps' home system, seems the end is getting close. We've been assigned to capture and consolidate a beachhead on their largest moon, open up to launch a proper assault on some of their industry there. Drop's in a few hours, hope that combat's better than sitting on this flying shitheap any longer.

Beachhead is secure. Damn Imps put up a tough fight.

### Day 4
Rickenbacker's done for. We got the news over the wire, clean shot right through the drive from a ground-to-orbit defense battery. Next to no time to get to the pods, as good as lost with all hands. Had a lot of friends up there. Guess we'll need to make it up to them.
